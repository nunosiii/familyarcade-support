Family Arcade

Support & Help

Family Arcade is a set of party games for Apple TV: Pixel Karts, Bumper Sumo, Bible Quest, Draw & Guess and Mini Golf. Everyone plays together using their iPhones as controllers, or a Siri Remote or game controller. No accounts, no ads.

Need help or have a question? Email raulmartinez.code@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 1 to 2 business days.

Frequently asked

How do I join a game with my iPhone?

Open Family Arcade on your Apple TV and pick a game. Then open Family Arcade on your iPhone and tap Find your TV. Your iPhone and Apple TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. When iOS asks to find devices on your local network, tap Allow.

Can I use a game controller or the Siri Remote?

Yes. Pair an Xbox or PlayStation controller with your Apple TV, then press A in a game's lobby to join, or click the Siri Remote. Each lobby shows a map of what the buttons do in that game.

My iPhone can't find the Apple TV.

Check both are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that Local Network access is on for Family Arcade in the iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Local Network.

Do I need an iPhone for every player?

No. Players can mix iPhones, game controllers and the Siri Remote. Draw & Guess needs an iPhone for the person drawing.

I found a bug or have feedback.

Email us with as much detail as you can, including your device and the steps that led to it. We read every message.
