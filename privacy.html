Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

The short version: Family Arcade does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your devices.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. Family Arcade has no accounts, no logins, no ads, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your devices

Player names, Bible Quest progress (which questions you've already seen) and settings such as music volume are stored only on your Apple TV or iPhone. They never leave the device and are removed if you delete the app.

Local network

The iPhone app uses your local Wi-Fi network only to find and talk to your Apple TV, so it can act as a game controller. Game input travels directly between your own devices on your home network and never goes over the internet.

Children

The app does not collect any data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
